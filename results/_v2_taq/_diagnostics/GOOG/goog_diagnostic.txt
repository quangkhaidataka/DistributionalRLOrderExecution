  Evidence:
   • CHECK 1: the GOOG negative mean is ~100% the TIMING term (IQN −35.6 = timing −36.0 + impact +0.21
     + penalty +0.22). Impact & penalty are tiny-positive and IDENTICAL to the AAPL reference
     (AAPL impact +1.1) — the learned agents are NOT executing more cheaply; they book price-timing P&L.
   • CHECK 2: on GOOG the learned agents HOLD inventory far longer (remain ≈0.2–0.4 mid-episode, 0.09 at
     step 19) vs AAPL front-loading to a flat 0.10 by step 4 — the holding is what enables the timing.
   • CHECK 5: GOOG Oct–Dec has a small MEAN linear window-drift (−2.57 bps) but ENORMOUS dispersion
     (std 119.5 bps/ep vs AAPL 40, MSFT 35) — the raw material for intraday timing.
   • CHECK 3: the negative mean does NOT collapse under per-episode DETRENDING (−36.7) or DAY-REVERSAL
     (−34.8). ⇒ the exploited structure is intraday VOLATILITY / mean-reversion (the agent sells into
     observed up-moves via Δp*), NOT a removable linear drift. This makes the confound MORE pervasive,
     not less: it cannot be detrended away.
   • CHECK 4: no bug, no look-ahead, no residual split — the effect is a real property of the policy+data,
     not a computation error.
  Interpretation: the learned agents behave volatility-ADAPTIVELY — on low-vol AAPL/MSFT they front-load
  (≈MaxSpeed, timing ≈0, IS ≈ +impact) but on high-vol GOOG they hold and time, conditioning on the observed
  price (Δp*) to sell into transient strength. That yields a large negative IS that is DIRECTIONAL/timing
  P&L, not cheaper liquidation. TWAP/MaxSpeed cannot time, so GOOG's tail "win" (CVaR95 27 vs MaxSpeed 130)
  reflects timing EXPOSURE to GOOG's volatile path, not superior execution.
  RECOMMENDATION: DEMOTE GOOG from a headline execution win to a Limitations/caveat —
  "on a highly volatile name the learned policies exploit intraday price structure through the price
   feature, generating timing P&L that confounds the pure execution-cost comparison; the confound is not
   removed by detrending, so GOOG is unsuitable as a clean cheaper-execution result." The AAPL/MSFT
  comparisons (impact-dominated, timing ≈0) remain valid execution results.
